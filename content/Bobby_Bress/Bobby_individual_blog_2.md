### Phase 2 Blog Post

For Phase 3, my main contributions were the ER diagrams and the wireframes. To make the ER diagrams, I discussed with Ari during class time how we would want the data to be stored. We decided to have a relationship between stats and stat type so that different stats and stat types could be added easily. We also decided on allowing the journalist to add their articles and for the ability for comments to be made on graphs so that the user could have a more interactive experience with the website. I added all of this in the ER diagrams. Then, I made rough drafts for what the interface could look like in the wireframes. Since we haven't completely decided on both ML models yet, the drafts aren't final, but they provide a good base for what they could look like in the future. I then worked on the DDL SQL code for our database design. I just used our diagram as an outline to write the code.

Outside of the project, I found our talk on EU tech policy and regulation very interesting. I was happy that I got to present on it, as I found it to be one of the more interesting talks that we've had. He brought up many interesting topics that I didn't know before, like how the US has control over who European companies are allowed to trade with.
