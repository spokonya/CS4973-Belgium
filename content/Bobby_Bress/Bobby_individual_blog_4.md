### Phase 4 Blog Post

This week, we mainly focused on making the finishing touches on the website. This was mainly in the energy trader persona, since it was the most recent one that we had made and was the least built out. In class, we had others look at our UI, and we analyzed them using the website in order to see areas that could be improved. They seemed to have some difficulty in navigating the site effectively, and many of the words were cut off as they didn't fit inside the boxes that they were supposed to. Due to this, we made changes to the button placement, the graph labels, the trade journal interface, and made sure no text on any of the pages was cut off. We also spent time going through each page more carefully to catch any remaining inconsistencies in the layout and spacing. We also generally made the interface better looking, improving the overall visual consistency and making sure the styling felt cohesive across the different sections of the energy trader persona.

Then, I redid the ER diagrams based on the new database model that Ari had designed. This involved making sure the diagrams accurately reflected the updated structure and relationships between the tables. We added this back to the second blog post so that it was up to date with our current progress. We also made sure that the database was connected to the energy trader, ensuring that the data was flowing correctly.
