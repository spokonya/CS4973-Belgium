# First Week in Belgium

In just one week in Belgium, I've explored historic cities, immersed myself in new course material, and embraced every experience this program has had to offer. Among the many experiences I’ve had with our Dialogue group, I’ve especially enjoyed exploring Leuven and learning about the beguinage on our Leuven walking tour, and seeing the beautiful Grand Place in Central Brussels. I also had the opportunity to try a Belgian waffle, which was delicious! I’ve also enjoyed Outside of exploring, I have also been learning tons of new concepts in DS3000. I really like math and have taken linear algebra in high school, so I’ve enjoyed getting reacquainted with it again.

In terms of the Phase 1 deliverable, I worked with Anjali to ideate and create 3 user personas and their stories.
