# Week Four Speakers/Activities

This week we had our final group of speakers, which included FEPS (a left-leaning party), Energy Ville, and a professor who covered smart city efforts within Leuven. At FEPS we got to hear the opposite political views from the right-leaning think tank we previously attended. Energy Ville provided the most in-depth insights into what cutting-edge green energy efforts look like within the EU. The Leuven smart city topics were super interesting too, and after living here for the past couple of weeks, the problems they talked about wanting to solve definitely resonated significantly more than they would have if the talk had been about some random city.

Additionally, we attended chocolate making and an escape room, which were both super fun activities that allowed for a nice break from the end-of-project ramp up, as well as some good laughs with friends!

# My Contributions

For this last update I spent a lot of time wiring the rest of the app's routes to make the remaining pages functional with the DB instead of using static data. Additionally, I made changes to the UI based on our user feedback rounds in class and in preparation for our practice presentation earlier in the week, which was another source of helpful info. I also built out the new relational database schema that was updated in the past blog posts and that we used to create our slides for our upcoming final presentation.
