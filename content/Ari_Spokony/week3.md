# Week Three Speakers/Activities

This past week we traveled to France and spent time in Strasbourg as well as Luxembourg. While away from Belgium we had a bit of a break from speakers, though in Luxembourg we visited Eurostat, a particularly relevant stop given how central it is to many of our projects. The speakers covered how they collect data (primarily through surveys), the timeline from question design to publication, who directs the topics Eurostat researchers investigate, and many other details about the institution's history and day-to-day operations.

# My Contributions

This week I revisited work from our first two weeks and made several edits that had been blocking our progress toward Phase III. This included revamping our user personas to introduce more variety across the three types and rewriting the user stories to motivate a fuller set of CRUD operations. I also translated our ER diagrams into finalized SQL, which is now supporting our first implemented ML model, and the wired routes for which I was also responsible for writing. At the moment we have the required number of routes, though these will likely be refined in the coming week as we finish the project and polish everything up.
