# Week Two Speakers/Activities

This week was packed with visits across Brussels, including a trip to the Commission, a talk from a NATO expert, and a session on European data protection law and AI. These talks were of particular note since my project group was responsible for giving a summary discussion highlighting three speakers (two of which are mentioned above, plus a representative of a right-wing political group).

I personally found the political group speaker most interesting, as he offered a perspective on the EU landscape that felt more candid than what official EU representatives typically provide. The conversation covered a wide range of topics, but most notably he shared insight into U.S.–EU relations and how they have shifted in recent years.

# My Contributions

This week I was a bit under the weather, so my contributions were lighter than last week. My main points of focus were the SQL schema, ER diagrams, and team blog post. Bobby and I worked together in class to flesh out the ER model and schema based on our proposed ML models and the CRUD operations our users will perform.

The current diagrams definitely need more work before submission, but as we move into next week we are confident we will have a clearer grasp on our project and the exact data our models and personas will require.
