## Hi, I'm Anjali

### From Philadelphia to Leuven

I am a rising second-year at Northeastern studying Finance and Data Science. I spent freshman year in Boston and am from Philadelphia. On campus I am involved with Women in Finance, the Private Equity and Venture Capital Club, and TAMID. This dialogue is my first time learning how data shows up in European policy—not just in a classroom back home.

### Phase I — personas

Our team is building an EU Energy Security Dashboard: a country-level view that forecasts prices and scores vulnerability to supply shocks. For Phase I, Rayna and I developed three personas—Lena (German homeowner), Marco (energy journalist in Brussels), and Sofia (policy analyst)—plus user stories for each.

### Favorite moment so far

The EU Commission visit stood out. Giulia Geneletti's talk on the Digital Services Act—especially protections for minors—made abstract regulation feel concrete. I have also loved Leuven's college-town rhythm, Brussels on the weekends, and the weather that can flip twice before lunch.
